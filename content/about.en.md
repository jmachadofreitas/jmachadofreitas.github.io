I’m an applied data scientist with 8+ years of experience across industrial AI, applied research, and data-driven decision support.

Most recently, I worked on predictive monitoring in industrial manufacturing, designing experiments, benchmarking approaches on heterogeneous data-scarce tabular datasets, and also communicating methods and findings to industrial stakeholders. My published research covers data-scarce industrial machine learning, invariant representation learning, and interpretable machine learning for microbiome analysis.

My focus is increasingly on building analytics systems and data-intensive applications, bringing analytical methods and machine learning into operational software. I’m particularly interested in work that connects applied data science with software and product development, and in communicating their value clearly to stakeholders to inform decision-making and drive business outcomes.
